Questions people ask

What is the best AudioPen alternative?
There is no single best one; it depends on why you are looking. If you want dictation into every app, look at a dictation tool. If you want meeting notes, look at a meeting assistant. If you like what AudioPen does but want an app that helps you think after the transcript, with no account and a pay-once option, Vox is built for that.
Is there a free AudioPen alternative?
Vox has a free plan that stays free: 10 recordings a day (after that, 1-minute takes), one file a day in full, and one full speaker-separation session a month. New users also get 14 days of Pro as a gift, with no card.
Is there an AudioPen alternative with a lifetime plan?
Vox Pro can be bought once for $149. It is also $6 a month or $59 a year. On November 10, 2026, the AudioPen pricing page listed $33 for 3 months, $99 for a year and $159 for 2 years, with no lifetime option.
Will Vox rewrite my notes in my own writing style?
Not the way AudioPen does. AudioPen lets you create custom writing styles from a sample. Vox has ready-made lenses, including a clean email and flashcards, and its focus is helping you think: decisions, plans, questions, counter-arguments.
Can I move my AudioPen notes to Vox?
There is no importer. You can paste text into a Vox note, or drop audio and video files into Vox to transcribe them.
Do I need an account or a card to try Vox?
No account and no card. Open it in your browser and talk. Your first transcriptions are on us; after that, Vox asks for your own AI key, and a Groq key is free.
