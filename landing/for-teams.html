What you get, and what you don't

Vox is built for one person thinking out loud. The team plans put that same tool on more devices. They do not turn it into a shared workspace.

You get
  • Everything in Vox Pro, on up to 20 or 50 devices.
  • One license key and one bill for the whole team.
  • The buyer can see and remove activated devices.
  • Priority e-mail support: messages from Team and Business customers are answered first.
  • Your own AI key: the company chooses the AI service, and the audio goes straight to it.
  • Each person can lock a world of notes with a password, encrypted on their device.
You don't get
  • A shared workspace. Colleagues do not see each other's notes.
  • Notes synced between devices or people.
  • An admin panel inside the app, or company single sign-on.
  • Usage reports per person.
  • A HIPAA-compliant service.
No account. Your notes are stored on your device. With your own key, audio goes straight to the AI service you chose. Your first transcriptions are on us and go through our small bridge — see the Privacy Policy.

Questions people ask

How many people is 20 devices?
Vox counts devices, not people. Most people use two or three (a phone, a computer, maybe a tablet), so Team usually covers 7 to 10 people and Business covers 17 to 25.
How does one license work for a whole team?
You buy once and get one license key. Each person pastes that key into Vox on their own devices. Every device counts as one activation, up to the limit of your plan.
Someone left the company. How do I remove their devices?
The person who bought the license can see every activated device in the customer portal of Freemius, our payment processor, and deactivate any of them. That frees the slot for someone else.
Can colleagues see each other's notes?
No. Vox has no shared workspace. Each person's notes are stored on their own device. To pass a note to a colleague, they export it or send it with Vox Transfer.
Who pays for the AI?
Vox runs on your own AI key. A company can give everyone the same company key, so the company chooses the AI service and pays that bill directly, or each person can use their own. Groq comes pre-selected and has a free key.
Is there a lifetime option for teams?
No. Team and Business are subscriptions, monthly or annual. The one-time $149 lifetime option is for Vox Pro, the personal plan with up to 6 devices.
We need more than 50 devices.
Write to support@voxcharmai.com and tell us how many devices you need.
We work with patients or clients. Is Vox right for us?
That depends on your legal and professional duties, and no pricing page should answer it for you. Vox is not offered as a HIPAA-compliant service. Please read the Terms of Use and the Privacy Policy, and check the confidentiality badge Vox shows for each AI service, before you record anyone.
