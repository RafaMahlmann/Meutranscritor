Questions people ask

How much do I earn?
35% of every payment made by a customer you bring: the first one and every renewal, for as long as they stay subscribed. On today's list prices that is about $2.10 a month on the monthly plan, $20.65 a year on the annual plan, or $52.15 once on the lifetime plan.
How long does my link count?
90 days. If someone clicks your link and buys Vox Pro within 90 days, the sale is yours.
Do I have to be a Vox customer?
No. But please use Vox before you recommend it. It is free, with no account, so trying it costs you nothing.
Who can join?
Anyone with an audience that would use Vox: people who think out loud, people with dyslexia or ADHD, students, writers, founders, note-taking and productivity fans. Every application is read by a person and approved one by one.
Where can I share my link?
On your own site, blog, newsletter, YouTube channel and social media. Not allowed: bulk or unsolicited e-mail. Commissions are for new customers only, so your own purchase does not count.
How do I get paid?
Sales are tracked by Freemius, the company that processes Vox payments. You see your clicks, sales and balance in your affiliate dashboard there. Payouts are sent by PayPal.
What can I say about privacy?
Please use this sentence, because it is the accurate one: "No account. Your notes are stored on your device. With your own key, audio goes straight to the AI service you chose. Your first transcriptions go through Vox's small bridge." Please do not say "nothing leaves your device" or "no servers"; that is not true.
