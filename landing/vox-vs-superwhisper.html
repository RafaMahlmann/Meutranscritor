Vox vs Superwhisper: write faster, or think it through?

Superwhisper is voice to text in any app: you speak, and the edited text is pasted where you are working. Vox is where you think out loud: it writes every word, then helps you decide, plan and untangle it.

No account. No card. Works in your browser.

The short answer

One replaces your keyboard. The other is a place to think. They overlap less than the names suggest.

Choose Superwhisper if
  • You want to speak instead of type in Slack, Gmail, your code editor or any other app.
  • You need transcription that works without internet.
  • You want native apps on Mac, Windows, iOS and Android.
  • You want modes that set the tone and format of the text, including ones you build yourself.
Choose Vox if
  • You talk to work out a decision or a plan, not to fill a text box.
  • You want lenses that push back: Devil's advocate, pre-mortem, Socratic questions.
  • You record conversations and need to know who said what.
  • You want nothing to install and no account to create.
  • You want the lower pay-once price.

Questions people ask

Is Vox a Superwhisper alternative?
Not for dictation. Superwhisper turns your voice into text in any app, and can work without internet. Vox does neither. Vox is for one person thinking out loud: it writes down what you said and then helps you decide, plan and question it. Pick by the job, and some people will want both.
Which one is cheaper?
On November 10, 2026, the Superwhisper site listed a free plan and Pro at $8.49 a month, $84.99 a year, or $249.99 one time. Vox has a free plan and Pro at $6 a month, $59 a year, or $149 once. They do different jobs, so compare what you would use, not only the price.
Do both have a lifetime plan?
Yes. Superwhisper lists Pro as a one-time purchase at $249.99. Vox Pro is $149 once.
Does Vox work without internet, like Superwhisper?
No. Superwhisper says it works offline, and that its offline models run best on Apple Silicon Macs. Out of the box, Vox sends your audio to the AI service you chose, so it needs a connection. Vox also has an optional local transcriber you can install on a computer, but it is a separate setup. If you must transcribe with no internet, Superwhisper is the one to look at.
Can I dictate into other apps with Vox?
No. Vox keeps your words in its own notes. From there you can copy the text, or run a lens to turn it into a clean email or a plan and paste that where you need it.
What is the "own key" part of Vox?
Vox uses your own key for the AI service that transcribes your audio. Groq comes pre-selected, and a Groq key is free and needs no card. It is one extra setup step. Your first transcriptions are on us, so you can try Vox before you set anything up. Superwhisper includes its models in the plan, and its Pro plan also lets you use your own AI API keys.
