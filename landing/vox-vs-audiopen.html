Vox vs AudioPen: clean text, or a clearer head?

Both start the same way: you talk, messy as it comes. AudioPen rewrites it into polished text. Vox writes it down, then helps you think it through — what you decided, what is still open, where the plan has a hole.

The short answer

Both are good tools. They are built for different moments.

Choose AudioPen if
  • You want your ramble rewritten in a writing style you pick, or in your own voice.
  • You want native apps on iPhone, Android, Mac and Windows.
  • You want to voice type into any other app.
  • You want notes sent on to other tools with Zapier.
Choose Vox if
  • You think out loud, and you want help deciding, planning and untangling.
  • You want lenses that push back: Devil's advocate, pre-mortem, Socratic questions.
  • You don't want to create an account, and you want your notes on your own device.
  • You would like to pay once and be done.
  • You record conversations and need to know who said what.

Questions people ask

Is Vox a good AudioPen alternative?
It depends on what you want after you stop talking. AudioPen rewrites what you said into clean text in a style you choose, and it has native apps and voice typing in other apps. Vox writes down what you said and then helps you think about it: decisions, next steps, the holes in your plan. If you mostly want polished writing, AudioPen is a strong choice. If you want to think something through, try Vox.
Does Vox have a lifetime plan?
Yes. Vox Pro is $149 once, or $6 a month, or $59 a year. On November 10, 2026, the AudioPen pricing page listed Prime at $33 for 3 months, $99 for a year and $159 for 2 years, with no lifetime option.
Do I need an account to use Vox?
No. You open Vox in your browser and start. Your notes are stored on your device.
What is the "own key" part?
Vox uses your own key for the AI service that transcribes your audio. Groq comes pre-selected, and a Groq key is free and needs no card. It is one extra setup step that AudioPen does not ask for. In exchange, with your own key, the audio goes straight to the service you chose. Your first transcriptions are on us, so you can try Vox before you set anything up.
Can Vox tell who said what in a recording?
Yes. Vox can separate speakers in a conversation (this is called diarization). The free plan includes one full session a month. AudioPen says on its own site that it is built for individual thought and does not label who said what.
Does Vox have a phone app?
Vox is a web app. It runs in the browser on your phone, tablet and computer, and you can install it from the browser. There is no App Store or Google Play app, and no keyboard that types into other apps. AudioPen has both.
