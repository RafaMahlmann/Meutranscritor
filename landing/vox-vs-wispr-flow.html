Vox vs Wispr Flow: type faster, or think clearer?

Wispr Flow is dictation: you speak, and polished text lands in whatever app you are in. Vox is for the thinking before the writing: you talk it through, and Vox helps you decide, plan and untangle it.

The short answer

Dictation apps type for you. Vox thinks with you. Pick by the job.

Choose Wispr Flow if
  • You already know what you want to say and want it typed fast, in any app.
  • You want dictation on Mac, Windows, iOS and Android.
  • You want a personal dictionary and snippets for names and jargon.
  • Your company needs SSO, admin controls or a signed BAA.
Choose Vox if
  • You don't know yet what you want to say, and talking is how you find out.
  • You want lenses that push back: Devil's advocate, pre-mortem, Socratic questions.
  • You want your spoken notes kept, organised and searchable in one place.
  • You don't want to create an account.
  • You would like to pay once and be done.

Questions people ask

Is Vox a Wispr Flow alternative?
Not for dictation. Wispr Flow types what you say into any app on your computer or phone, and it is good at it. Vox does not type into other apps. Vox is for the moment before the writing: you talk a thought through, Vox writes it down, and its lenses help you decide what you actually think. Many people could use both.
Can I dictate into other apps with Vox?
No. Vox keeps your words in its own notes. From there you can copy the text, or run a lens to turn it into a clean email or a plan and paste that where you need it.
How much does Wispr Flow cost compared to Vox?
On November 10, 2026, the Wispr Flow pricing page listed a free plan with a weekly word limit, Pro at $15 per user a month (or $12 per user a month on the annual plan) and Growth at $23 (or $18 annual). Vox Pro is $6 a month, $59 a year, or $149 once.
Does Vox have a lifetime plan?
Yes, $149 once. The Wispr Flow pricing page did not list a lifetime option on November 10, 2026.
Do I need an account to use Vox?
No. You open Vox in your browser and start. Your notes are stored on your device.
What is the "own key" part?
Vox uses your own key for the AI service that transcribes your audio. Groq comes pre-selected, and a Groq key is free and needs no card. It is one extra setup step that Wispr Flow does not ask for. Your first transcriptions are on us, so you can try Vox before you set anything up.
