Why people look for an alternative

Otter does its job well. These are reasons one person, working alone, might still want something else.

"I don't need a bot in my calls"

Vox never joins a call

Otter joins Zoom, Microsoft Teams and Google Meet to write and share notes. Vox only hears what you record yourself, or the file you bring.

"It is just me"

Vox is built for one person

Otter's plans are priced per user and organised around a workspace. Vox has no workspace and no seats: one person, one app.

"I keep an eye on the minutes"

Vox does not sell minutes

Otter Basic includes 300 minutes a month and 30 minutes per conversation; Pro includes 1,200 and 90. Vox Free limits recordings per day, and Pro lifts that limit.

"A summary is not enough"

Vox helps you think

After the transcript you pick a lens: Help me decide, Devil's advocate, pre-mortem, Socratic questions, Vent → clarity. Some of them push back.

"I have old recordings"

Files work on the free plan

Drop in audio or video: one file a day in full on Vox Free. Otter Basic allows 3 file imports in total; Otter Pro allows 10 a month.

"I would like to pay once"

Vox has a lifetime plan

$149 once, or $6 a month, or $59 a year. Otter Pro is a subscription: $16.99 per user a month, or $8.33 on the annual plan.

Questions people ask

What is a good Otter alternative if I don't need a meeting bot?
It depends on what you used Otter for. If you still need notes from team calls, a meeting assistant is the right kind of tool and Otter is a strong one. If you found that most of your recordings were you talking to yourself, working out a decision or a plan, Vox is built for that: it writes down what you said and then helps you think about it.
Is there a free Otter alternative?
Vox has a free plan that stays free: 10 recordings a day (from the 11th on, 1-minute takes), one file a day in full, and one full speaker-separation session a month. New users also get 14 days of Pro as a gift, with no card. Otter has a free plan too: on November 10, 2026, its pricing page listed Basic with 300 transcription minutes a month and up to 30 minutes per conversation.
Can Vox record my Zoom, Teams or Google Meet call?
Not by joining it. Vox has no bot and does not enter calls. You can record a conversation in the room with Vox, or drop in an audio or video recording you already have, and Vox will transcribe it and separate who said what.
Is there an Otter alternative with no monthly fee?
Vox Pro can be bought once for $149. It is also $6 a month or $59 a year. On November 10, 2026, the Otter pricing page listed Pro at $16.99 per user a month, or $8.33 per user a month on the annual plan, and no pay-once option.
Can I move my Otter notes to Vox?
There is no importer. You can paste text into a Vox note, or drop audio and video files into Vox to transcribe them again.
Do I need an account or a card to try Vox?
No account and no card. Open it in your browser and talk. Your first transcriptions are on us. After that, Vox asks for your own AI key, which is one extra setup step; a Groq key is free and needs no card.
