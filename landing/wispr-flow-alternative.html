When to stay with Wispr Flow

We would rather you choose well than choose us.

  • You want to speak instead of type in email, chat and every other app. Vox does not do that.
  • You want native apps on Mac, Windows, iOS and Android.
  • You rely on a dictionary and snippets for names and jargon.
  • You dictate in many languages: Wispr Flow lists more than 100.
  • Your company needs a shared workspace, single sign-on or a signed BAA.
  • You don't want to set up an AI key, ever.

Questions people ask

What is a good Wispr Flow alternative if I want more than dictation?
First, be sure dictation is not what you need. If you want speech typed into every app, Wispr Flow does exactly that and Vox does not. If what you want is help with the thinking itself, Vox is a different kind of tool: it keeps what you said as a note and runs lenses on it, such as Help me decide or Devil's advocate.
Does Vox replace Wispr Flow?
For most people, no. Vox does not type into other apps. Some people use a dictation app to write and Vox to think. If you only ever used Wispr Flow to talk ideas out and then tidy them up, Vox may be all you need.
Is there a free Wispr Flow alternative?
Vox has a free plan that stays free: 10 recordings a day (from the 11th on, 1-minute takes) and one file a day in full. New users also get 14 days of Pro as a gift, with no card. Wispr Flow has a free plan too; on November 10, 2026, its pricing page described it as limited to a number of words per week.
Is there a Wispr Flow alternative I can pay for once?
Vox Pro is $149 once, or $6 a month, or $59 a year. On November 10, 2026, the Wispr Flow pricing page listed Pro at $15 per user a month, or $12 on the annual plan, and no pay-once option.
Can Vox transcribe a recording I already have?
Yes. Drop an audio or video file into Vox. On the free plan, one file a day is transcribed in full. Vox can also separate who said what.
Do I need an account or a card to try Vox?
No account and no card. Open it in your browser and talk. Your first transcriptions are on us. After that, Vox asks for your own AI key, which is one extra setup step; a Groq key is free and needs no card.
