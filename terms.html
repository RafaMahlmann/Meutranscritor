What Vox is

Vox is a tool that runs in your browser. It records or imports audio, sends it to a speech-to-text service you choose to turn it into text, and can apply AI tools ("lenses" and "recipes") to that text. Your notes are stored on your device. Vox doesn't keep your recordings and doesn't train AI with them.

With your own key, your audio and text go from your device straight to the service you chose. We don't receive them. With the local transcriber, the audio doesn't leave your computer. The exceptions — your first free transcriptions and lenses, optional usage counts, and buying Vox Pro — are explained in the Privacy Policy.

  • Ask before you start, and tell people an AI service will turn the recording into text.
  • Don't record anyone in secret, and don't record conversations you have no right to record, including confidential or privileged ones you aren't part of.
