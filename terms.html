What Vox is

Vox is a client-side tool that runs in your browser to record or import audio, transcribe it using a speech-to-text provider you configure, and apply optional AI-powered transformations ("lenses" and "recipes") to the resulting text. Your notes live on your device. Vox has no server that stores your content: your audio and text go only to the providers you choose. The single, optional exception is described in Your first transcriptions and lenses, on us.

Your first transcriptions and lenses, on us

Before you set up a key of your own, your first transcriptions and your first lenses can be made with Vox's own key, through a small bridge we run (details in the Privacy Policy). They are a free courtesy, offered as they are: how many there are and how long each one can be may change, and they may be paused at any time — for example, when the day's limit is reached. Nothing you record is lost when that happens: the recording waits on your device until you add a key.
